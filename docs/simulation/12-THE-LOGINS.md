# Signing in as every person in the run

🔴 **Generated by `npm run logins` from `scripts/_cast.ts`. Do not edit it by hand.**

The six months of records stay on the production database when the run ends. They are
not deleted and not restored away, so every person below can be signed in as, and their
record read, for as long as the data is kept.

One password for all of them:

    Simulation2026!

The address is always the person's own name: `<first name>.<surname>@example.com`, lower
case. `npm run on:production -- verify:cast` reads every one of them back out of the
database and checks the password actually opens it, which is a different fact from the
row existing and the one that goes wrong.

## The one with no login

`P6` Ziad Example never creates an account. He sees a therapist three times through join
links and stays a stranger to us. His record is reachable from his clinician's side and
from nowhere else, and that is the product working rather than a gap in this table.
`verify:cast` fails if an account for him ever appears.

## The payroll is not in here

Seven people are on `/admin/actuals` as employees and only three of them have logins: the
operator and the two who share the transfer queue. The other four are a salary line and
nothing else, because nothing in the run needs them to press a button.
