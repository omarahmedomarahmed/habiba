# 52.5 — the English voiceover, timed

One block per screen, in cut order, matching `EDIT-LIST.md`. Timings are **read times at 150 words
per minute**, the pace of somebody explaining something they believe rather than reading ad copy.
Each block is written to fit inside its frame's hold with a beat at each end.

Where a line also appears as on-screen text in the edit list it is marked **[card]**. A line marked
[card] is spoken **or** shown, never both.

## Notes for whoever records this

* **Do not sell.** Every line above is a statement of fact about a screen that is on the picture. The
  moment a line describes something the frame does not show, cut the line rather than the frame.
* **The pauses are where the argument is.** 1.6, 2.9 and 4.3 each need a full beat of silence after
  them. They are the three places somebody watching realises the product does something they assumed
  it did not.
* **Names are said as written.** Layla Demo. Tarek Example. They are meant to sound synthetic; a
  reader smoothing them into ordinary names is undoing C225 out loud.
* **Total across four cuts: 6 minutes 55 seconds** of voiceover against 6 minutes 55 seconds of
  frames. No cut runs long enough to need music to hold it together.
