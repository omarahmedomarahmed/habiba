# 24Therapy Research 2: Safety, Records, Consent, Privacy, Ops, UX, API, Accessibility

Research date: 23 September 2026. Scope: current (2024 to 2026) best practice for the patient app, clinician workspace, clinic owner portal, employer portal, partner API portal and internal operations console. Launch market Egypt (Arabic first, RTL) with a US entity.

Where a vendor does not publish a number (for example employer reporting thresholds), this document says so, rather than guessing.

## 1. Crisis and safety UX

- **Placement and reach.** A 2026 crisis support assessment framework for mental health apps scores apps on navigation depth (number of interactions to reach crisis resources), multiple pathways to them, one-tap calling, offline availability, localized numbers, displayed hotline operating hours, and screen reader support. Only 15% of 302 commercial mental health apps included 988, and common failures were buried navigation, broken links and no offline access. Sources: [Frontiers in Digital Health 2026, MHACSAF](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1814547/full), [Psychiatric Services, Mental Health Apps and 988](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ps.20240485).
- **Never blocked by.** Following from those criteria: the crisis entry must work signed out, without payment, without network (numbers cached locally, `tel:` links), without completing onboarding, and during a live video session. WCAG 2.2 SC 3.2.6 Consistent Help also requires help mechanisms to appear in the same relative place on every page. Sources: [Frontiers MHACSAF](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1814547/full), [W3C, What's new in WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/).
- **Safe messaging.** Say "died by suicide", never "committed" or "successful/failed attempt". No method or location detail. Do not frame suicide as an epidemic or unsolvable. Always pair content with hope, concrete action and a resource. WHO's 2023 update adds specific guidance for online and digital content. Sources: [Action Alliance, Tips for messaging safely](https://suicidepreventionmessaging.org/safety/tips-messaging-safely), [Action Alliance, Messaging don'ts](https://suicidepreventionmessaging.org/safety/messaging-donts), [WHO, Preventing suicide: resource for media professionals 2023](https://who.int/publications/i/item/9789240076846).
- **PHQ-9 item 9.** A positive answer to item 9 strongly predicts suicide attempt: one-year attempt risk rose from 0.4% ("not at all") to 4% ("nearly every day"). The Joint Commission's NPSG 15.01.01 requires a validated screening tool used with its exact wording, a fuller assessment when the screen is positive (C-SSRS is named), and a documented mitigation plan matched to risk. Evidence-based follow-up is the Stanley-Brown Safety Plan (warning signs, coping steps, contacts, professionals and crisis lines, means safety). Sources: [Simon et al. 2013, Psychiatric Services](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24036589/), [Joint Commission R3 Report 18 (updated Dec 2025)](https://digitalassets.jointcommission.org/api/public/content/459bbe2be1ab4e5082b8a8d49d0c94e0?v=69255bb9), [SPRC, Stanley-Brown Safety Plan](https://sprc.org/resources/stanley-brown-safety-plan/).
- **Egypt lines, including their hours.** The General Secretariat of Mental Health psychological support lines are 08008880700 and 0220816831. The unified MoHP hotline 105 covers mental health and addiction, but Ahram Online gives its hours as Monday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm, so it is **not 24/7**. The 24/7 fallbacks are ambulance 123 and unified emergency 112. Sources: [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2020/04/01/health-ministry-launches-mental-health-support-hotlines-during-covid-19-pandemic/), [Ahram Online, 105 hotline](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/1236/549334/Egypt/Health/Egypt-Health-Ministry-transforms-its-hotline-into-.aspx), [U.S. Embassy Egypt, emergency numbers](https://eg.usembassy.gov/services/emergency-contact-information-for-u-s-citizens/), [Wikipedia, Telephone numbers in Egypt (112 since 2022)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_numbers_in_Egypt).
- **Gulf, UK and US.** UAE 800HOPE (800 4673), 8am to 8pm, Arabic and English, call or WhatsApp. Saudi NCMH 920033360, 12 hours a day. Qatar 16000, 8am to 6pm Saturday to Thursday. UK: 999 for immediate risk, NHS 111 option 2 for urgent mental health (24/7), Samaritans 116 123 (24/7). US: 988 by call, text or chat, with interpreters in 240+ languages and wireless calls routed by location (FCC order, nationwide carriers by January 2025). Sources: [Emirates Foundation, 800HOPE](https://www.emiratesfoundation.ae/ef/hope), [Saudipedia, NCMH](https://saudipedia.com/en/article/567/government-and-politics/centers/national-center-for-mental-health-promotion), [Hamad Medical Corporation, 16000](https://hamad.qa/EN/news/2025/April/Pages/National-Mental-Health-Helpline-Celebrates-Milestone-in-Providing-Specialized-Support-Across-Qatar.aspx), [NHS, urgent help for mental health](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/mental-health-services/where-to-get-urgent-help-for-mental-health/), [SAMHSA 988 FAQ](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/988/faqs), [FCC 988 georouting](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-adopts-rules-requiring-georouting-all-wireless-calls-988-0).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Put one persistent "Get help now" control in the same spot on every patient screen (header or bottom bar), including the video room, and make it work signed out and offline. Choose the crisis list by the patient's current country, not their account country. For each number show whether it is open right now, and when no line is open, lead with 123/112 in Egypt. Store the list as versioned config so ops can update it without a release, and re-verify every number and its hours before launch. A PHQ-9 item 9 score of 1 or more should: show the crisis sheet immediately in the questionnaire flow, send a risk flag to the assigned clinician (or an on-call clinician in "therapist now"), and open a C-SSRS and safety plan task in the clinician workspace. Review all copy against safe messaging rules in both Arabic and English.

## 2. Clinical documentation integrity

- **Sign, then lock.** AHIMA recommends signing and locking encounter notes electronically as soon as possible. Any later change goes in as an amendment, addendum or late entry that carries the current date and time, the reason, and a signature, with a link or symbol on the original entry. Sources: [AHIMA, Amendments in the EHR Toolkit](https://www.ahima.org/media/r2dc4xhg/amendments-in-the-electronic-health-record_axs.pdf), [AHIMA, Information Integrity in the EHR](https://www.ahima.org/media/sxflfny0/information-integrity-in-the-electronic-health-record_axs.pdf).
- **Nothing is deleted.** Medicare contractors require every amendment, correction or delayed entry to be clearly and permanently marked as one, with its date and author, and all original content kept. Electronic signatures count only when the system has unique user credentials and a reliable audit trail. Sources: [Noridian, Documentation guidelines for amended records](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jeb/cert-reviews/mr/documentation-guidelines-for-amended-records), [Noridian, Signature requirements](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jddme/topics/documentation/signature-requirements).
- **How EHRs do it.** In Epic, changing a signed note turns it into status "Addendum", time-stamped with the new signature. The original stays visible in Chart Review. Sources: [University of Iowa Epic education, Editing and reviewing notes](https://epicsupport.sites.uiowa.edu/epic-resources/editing-reviewing-notes), [Practice Fusion, Addendum to a signed encounter](https://help.practicefusion.com/s/article/How-do-I-add-an-addendum-to-a-signed-encounter).
- **HIPAA right to amend (45 CFR 164.526).** Patients can request an amendment, but the covered entity decides. It must act within 60 days, with one 30-day extension. It can deny a request if the record is accurate and complete or was not created by the entity. An accepted amendment is appended or linked, not overwritten. A denied patient can file a statement of disagreement, which stays with the record. Sources: [eCFR 45 CFR 164.526](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-E/section-164.526).
- **Joint Commission.** RC.01.01.01 requires a complete and accurate record. RC.01.02.01 requires entries to be authenticated, with the time frame for authentication set by organization policy and law. Sources: [Joint Commission FAQ, Authentication time frame](https://www.jointcommission.org/standards/standard-faqs/hospital-and-hospital-clinics/record-of-care-treatment-and-services-rc/000001691/).
- **Mental health specifics.** Psychotherapy notes (45 CFR 164.501) must be kept separate from the medical record. They are excluded from the patient's right of access and from Cures Act "open notes", and disclosing them needs a separate authorization. Progress notes are shared with patients by default, and the "preventing harm" exception (for example acute suicidality) is narrow and must be documented case by case. Sources: [eCFR 45 CFR 164.501](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-E/section-164.501), [HHS, HIPAA and mental health information](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/hipaa-privacy-rule-and-sharing-info-related-to-mental-health.pdf), [Telehealth.org, Open notes and psychotherapy notes](https://telehealth.org/blog/open-notes-rule/).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Give each AI-drafted note three states: Draft (AI, editable by the clinician only), Signed (locked and hashed), and Amended (addenda only). Only the signing clinician can add an addendum. A supervisor can co-sign, and nobody, ops included, can edit or delete a signed note. Show "AI drafted, reviewed and signed by Dr X at time T" on the note, and keep the draft diff for audit. Build the patient's "own my record" view around a request-amendment flow that tracks the 60-day clock, lets the clinician accept or deny with a reason, and attaches the patient's statement of disagreement. Keep private psychotherapy notes as a separate object that never appears in exports, employer data, partner APIs or the patient view.

## 3. AI scribe and recording consent

- **Litigation risk is real (2025 to 2026).** A November 2025 class action alleges Sharp HealthCare used Abridge without all-party consent under California's Invasion of Privacy Act, the federal Wiretap Act and CMIA, and that the tool auto-inserted "patient consented" text into charts. Similar suits followed against Sutter and MemorialCare. Sources: [KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/health/2025/12/11/lawsuit-claims-sharp-healthcare-secretly-recorded-exam-room-conversations-without-patient-consent), [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/news-insights/new-class-action-targets-healthcare-ai-recordings.html), [Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/patients-sue-two-more-health-systems-over-ai-scribe-use-lack-2026a1000bwq).
- **How vendors handle it.** Abridge deployments (for example Johns Hopkins) require the clinician to get patient consent before recording. DAX leaves consent workflows (opt-in, opt-out, signage, documentation) to the deploying organization, and telehealth use requires consent to record. Upheal makes the provider responsible for collecting client consent, lets clients decline or withdraw at any time without affecting care, deletes recordings by default, and needs separate opt-in from both clinician and client before any data is used for model training. Sources: [Johns Hopkins IT, Abridge](https://it.johnshopkins.edu/ai/abridge-ai-scribe/), [Microsoft DAX Copilot FAQ](https://csocontent.nuance.com/Hub/DAX%20Copilot/Misc%20Resources/DAX-Copilot-FAQs.pdf), [Upheal, Collecting client consent](https://www.upheal.io/support/en/articles/8614351-collecting-client-consent), [Upheal, AI therapy notes and recordings](https://www.upheal.io/blog/ai-therapy-notes).
- **If the patient declines.** The session goes ahead unchanged. The clinician writes the note from a typed or dictated summary afterwards (Upheal's fallback), and the refusal is recorded as structured data. Commentators point out that simple signage or a checkbox is not informed consent, and that consent rates drop when disclosures are detailed. Sources: [Upheal vs Freed](https://www.upheal.io/comparisons/upheal-vs-freed), [KevinMD 2026, consent checkbox](https://kevinmd.com/2026/07/your-ambient-ai-scribe-consent-checkbox-is-not-consent.html), [AMA Journal of Ethics, Nov 2025](https://journalofethics.ama-assn.org/article/how-should-we-think-about-ambient-listening-and-transcription-technologies-influences-ehr/2025-11).
- **US law.** All-party consent states include California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania and Washington. Everyone whose voice is captured (patient, family, interpreter) must consent before recording starts. Since 1 January 2026, Texas TRAIGA requires plain-language disclosure of AI involvement by the date of service. Sources: [Transcribe Health, state-by-state guide](https://transcribe.health/en-us/blog/patient-consent-ai-scribe-recording), [KevinMD on TRAIGA](https://kevinmd.com/2026/07/your-ambient-ai-scribe-consent-checkbox-is-not-consent.html).
- **EU AI Act.** Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026, with disclosure due at first interaction at the latest and in an accessible form. An administrative scribe is usually MDR Class I, which is not "high risk" under the Act. If the tool suggests diagnoses or treatment it moves to Class IIa or above and becomes high risk. Sources: [EU AI Act Article 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/), [European Commission, Article 50 FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act), [Tandem Health, MDR and AI scribes](https://tandemhealth.ai/resources/knowledge/why-ai-scribes-need-to-fall-under-eu-mdr).
- **Egypt PDPL 151/2020.** The Executive Regulations (Ministerial Decision 81 of 2025) were issued on 1 November 2025, and the grace period ends around 31 October 2026. Health data is sensitive data, which needs explicit written consent (Art. 14), a license, and an accredited DPO. Cross-border transfers (for example to US cloud or AI vendors) need a separate transfer license from the Data Protection Centre. Sources: [Chambers, First look at Egypt's Executive Regulations](https://chambers.com/articles/a-first-look-at-egypt-s-personal-data-protection-executive-regulations), [Kennedys 2026](https://www.kennedyslaw.com/en/thought-leadership/article/2026/egypt-s-personal-data-protection-law-the-compliance-countdown-has-begun/), [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law_update_articles/from-policy-to-practice-egypt-issues-executive-regulations-of-the-personal-data-protection-law/), [Consortio, cross-border transfer](https://consortiolawfirm.com/cross-border-data-transfer-egypt-pdpl/).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Ask for recording and AI-drafting consent before the session, as two separate plain-Arabic toggles: "record audio" and "AI drafts note". Ask again in the pre-join screen and give both sides a visible "recording" indicator. Store consent as a signed event (who, what scope, version, timestamp), never as text the AI writes into the note. If either side declines, recording is off and the clinician gets a "write summary, AI formats it" path, so the session and the note still happen. Consent can be withdrawn mid-session, and doing so stops capture straight away. Keep AI output strictly to documentation (no diagnostic suggestions) to stay outside high-risk classification. Before any audio or text leaves Egypt, get the PDPL sensitive-data and cross-border licenses, or process it in-region.

## 4. Employer benefit privacy

- **What employers see.** Spring Health employers get de-identified, aggregated reporting (engagement, utilization, access, population-level clinical improvement) through Spring Insights. They never get member-level clinical data, notes, diagnoses or assessment answers without the member's express authorization. Modern Health shares only aggregated, anonymized data, except to prevent imminent harm or where the law requires. Sources: [Spring Health, PHI and workplace confidentiality](https://careteam.springhealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/24232901859860-Protected-Health-Information-PHI-and-Workplace-Confidentiality), [Spring Health FAQ](https://www.springhealth.com/faq), [Modern Health, Is my care confidential](https://support.modernhealth.com/hc/en-us/articles/5056344539675-Is-my-care-confidential-from-my-employer).
- **Names of enrolled employees.** Practice differs. For the meditation app, Headspace for Work has shown employers registration data (first name, last name, registration date, last-use date) while hiding usage. For Headspace Care (therapy) it says it shares no individual-level usage. Calm and Talkspace share only aggregates. Sources: [Mozilla Foundation 2022 review](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/blog/mental-health-awareness-2022-employer-access/), [Headspace Help, will my employer know](https://help.headspace.com/hc/en-us/articles/19796786801563-Will-my-employer-or-people-I-work-with-know-that-I-m-using-Headspace-Care).
- **Minimum group size.** Lyra, Spring Health, Modern Health and Headspace do not publish a numeric k threshold. Modern Health used expert-determination statistical de-identification (Privacy Analytics) rather than a fixed rule. Comparable published norms: Culture Amp suppresses groups below about 5, and CMS suppresses any cell of 1 to 10, including values that can be derived from percentages or combinations. Sources: [Privacy Analytics, Modern Health case study](https://privacy-analytics.com/resources/case-studies/modern-health-proactively-assesses-privacy-protections-for-member-data), [Culture Amp, confidentiality protections](https://support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/7048386-confidentiality-protections-in-reporting), [ResDAC, CMS cell size suppression](https://www.resdac.org/articles/cms-cell-size-suppression-policy).
- **Cadence and filters.** Modern Health Central and Spring Insights offer on-demand or "real-time" dashboards that can be segmented by department or location, which increases re-identification risk. EAP guidance says to agree the minimum group size before reporting starts, and to merge or suppress small groups. Sources: [Fierce Healthcare, Modern Health data tool](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/tech/modern-health-rolls-out-data-tool-for-employers-as-companies-ramp-up-efforts-to-address), [Modern Health Central](https://www.modernhealth.com/post/introducing-modern-health-central), [EAP reporting and confidentiality guide](https://www.mypsychology.my/eap-reporting-confidentiality-hr-malaysia/).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** The employer portal should never show names of people who used care (therapy use is the sensitive fact in itself). Eligibility files flow in only. Use k of at least 10 for any cell, following the CMS convention, which is stricter than survey tools because this is clinical data. Apply suppression to the complement too, so a small group cannot be worked out as "total minus others". Filters narrower than k collapse into "Other". Refresh dashboards monthly with a lag, not in real time, because small Egyptian employers make timing attacks easy. Put the k value and the "your employer never sees..." promise in the employee onboarding screen, and write both into the employer contract.

## 5. Operations console for manual money and PHI

- **Four eyes (maker-checker).** Split every sensitive action into a request by one person and approval by a different person, and never let someone approve their own request. NIST AC-5 also separates the people who administer access from the people who administer audit. Sources: [Wikipedia, Maker-checker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maker-checker), [NIST SP 800-53 AC-5](https://csf.tools/reference/nist-sp-800-53/r5/ac/ac-5/), [Opcito, Maker-checker implementation](https://www.opcito.com/blogs/maker-checker-implementation-guide-for-secure-fintech-systems).
- **Idempotent money actions.** Every mutating money call carries an idempotency key that is created and saved before the call. A replay returns the stored first result, including errors. Stripe keeps keys for 24 hours, so longer flows need a new key per attempt batch. Retries use exponential backoff with jitter. Sources: [Stripe API, Idempotent requests](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests), [Stripe blog, Designing robust APIs with idempotency](https://stripe.com/blog/idempotency).
- **Exception queues.** Match expected payments against bank or wallet records. Anything unmatched goes to a manual queue for review, grouped by type (duplicate, missing credit, settlement break, timing, data gap). Triage quickly and resolve at transaction level, since the AFP average is 6.1 business days per discrepancy. Sources: [Modern Treasury, Manually reconcile exceptions](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/reconciliation/docs/manually-reconcile-an-expected-payment), [Modern Treasury, Exception handling](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/payments/docs/exception-handling-manual-reconciliation), [Rexi, reconciliation exceptions](https://rexi.finance/blog/payment-reconciliation-software/payment-reconciliation-exceptions-types-causes-escalate.html).
- **Reason-required and audited.** Audit records must capture what happened, when, where, from what source, with what outcome, and who did it. HIPAA 164.312(b) requires recording and examining activity on ePHI systems, and OCR expects those logs to be reviewed regularly. Sources: [NIST SP 800-53 AU-3](https://csf.tools/reference/nist-sp-800-53/r4/au/au-3/), [HHS OCR, Security Rule guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/guidance/index.html), [For The Record, OCR audit controls](https://www.fortherecordmag.com/archives/0517p30.shtml).
- **Break-glass.** Emergency access must be time-limited, give a reason, alert on use, and be reviewed afterwards. Standing break-glass with weak logging is itself an audit finding. Sources: [Yale HIPAA, Break glass procedure](https://hipaa.yale.edu/security/break-glass-procedure-granting-emergency-access-critical-ephi-systems), [Censinet, Break-glass pros and cons](https://censinet.com/perspectives/break-glass-access-pros-and-cons-for-healthcare).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Refunds, payouts, manual InstaPay/Fawry confirmations and credit adjustments are all "requests" with a required reason code and free text, and a second approver whenever the amount is above a threshold. A payment can only mark Pending to Paid once, keyed on the rail's reference. The console's home screen is the exception queue (unmatched InstaPay transfers, expired Fawry references, double-paid bookings), not a dashboard. By default ops sees no PHI. Opening a patient's clinical data is break-glass: pick a reason, get a 30-minute grant, the patient's clinician and the privacy officer are notified, and it goes on a weekly review list. Write the audit log append-only, and make it readable only by people who cannot change access.

## 6. Patient app UX that reduces drop-off

- **Guest first.** Baymard finds 19% of shoppers abandon when forced to create an account, and 62% of sites hide their guest option. Gradual engagement (value first, account later) is the standard mobile pattern. Ask only for what the first session needs. Sources: [Baymard, Reduce cart abandonment](https://baymard.com/blog/reduce-cart-abandonment), [Appcues, Gradual engagement](https://www.appcues.com/blog/gradual-engagement-mobile-app-first-screen), [Qventive, Telehealth onboarding friction](https://qventive.com/patient-onboarding-to-telehealth-reducing-friction/).
- **Time to first session.** A longer wait before the appointment increases no-shows. Clients who attended intake after a reminder had lead times of four days or less, and text reminders reduce missed visits in randomized studies. This supports a "therapist now" path plus reminders. Sources: [Psychiatric Services, Text message reminders in community mental health](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ps.201100211), [Permanente Journal, targeted text reminders RCT](https://www.thepermanentejournal.org/doi/10.7812/TPP/21.078), [PMC, Telephone reminders in adult CMH](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13329279/).
- **Saved payment for Egypt.** Egypt's card tokenization rules let PSPs like Paymob store cards as tokens for one-tap repeat payment. Fawry reference codes and InstaPay remain essential for people without cards, but they are asynchronous, so the booking needs a clear "awaiting payment" state and a hold timer. Sources: [Lexology, Egypt tokenization regulation](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=4ade2855-28cb-47ee-a7ce-d4f81ed67c84), [Paymob online payments](https://paymob.com/en/online-payment), [Mrfqy, Fawry, COD and InstaPay guide](https://www.mrfqy.com/blog/fawry-cod-instapay-egypt).
- **RTL layout.** Mirror direction-dependent UI (back arrows, progress, carousels). Do not mirror media playback controls, clocks or numbers. Phone numbers stay LTR inside RTL text. Sources: [Material Design, Bidirectionality](https://m2.material.io/design/usability/bidirectionality.html), [Material 3, Bidirectionality and RTL](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/bidirectionality-rtl), [Apple HIG, Right to left](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/right-to-left), [Apple WWDC22, Design for Arabic](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10034/).
- **Arabic typography and numerals.** Use a real Arabic webfont, a slightly larger size than Latin, and body line-height of about 1.7 or more so diacritics are not clipped. W3C ALReq is the reference for justification and bidi. Egypt uses Eastern Arabic digits in daily life, and in current ICU/CLDR (tested: ICU 78.2, CLDR 48) `ar-EG` formats as ١٬٢٣٤٫٥ while bare `ar` and `ar-AE` now default to Latin digits, so always pass an explicit locale or `nu` extension. Pick one digit system per surface and do not mix. Sources: [W3C, Arabic and Persian Layout Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/), [W3C, Arabic typography gap analysis](https://w3c.github.io/typography/gap-analysis/arab-ar-fa), [Voxire, Arabic RTL typography 2026](https://voxire.com/blog/arabic-rtl-typography-web-design-2026/), [Wikipedia, Eastern Arabic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Arabic_numerals), [MDN, Intl.NumberFormat](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/NumberFormat).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Aim for about 5 taps to a first session on the "now" path: need, match, time, pay, join. Collect only a phone OTP at payment and turn the guest into an account afterwards. Card users get a saved token, and InstaPay/Fawry users get a booking held for a set time with a countdown. After booking, a session detail screen is the hub for everything (join button, time in the patient's zone, therapist, receipt, reschedule/cancel policy, recording consent state, crisis link), and every inbox or push notification deep-links to it. Send reminders by SMS or WhatsApp at 24 hours and 1 hour. Set numerals in `ar-EG` explicitly, pick one digit system for prices and times, and wrap phone numbers and codes in LTR isolates (`<bdi>` or `dir="ltr"`).

## 7. Partner and developer API portal

- **Key rotation.** Let multiple keys be active at once, and give a "roll key" action that issues a new key and sets an expiry on the old one (Stripe allows up to 7 days of overlap). Show last-used times so a key is only expired once its traffic reaches zero. Sources: [Stripe, API keys](https://docs.stripe.com/keys), [Zuplo, Zero-downtime key rotation](https://zuplo.com/learning-center/api-key-rotation-lifecycle-management).
- **Throttle, do not suspend.** Return 429 with `Retry-After`, and advertise limits with the IETF `RateLimit` and `RateLimit-Policy` fields (still a draft). A leaky-bucket limiter (as in Shopify) recovers by itself once the client slows down, and the account is not suspended for going over. Sources: [IETF draft-ietf-httpapi-ratelimit-headers](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpapi-ratelimit-headers/), [Shopify API rate limits](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/limits).
- **Webhook retries and a failed state.** Stripe retries in live mode with exponential backoff (5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 5 h, 10 h, then hourly) for up to 3 days, then disables the endpoint and notifies the owner. Retries in test mode are shorter. Standard Webhooks keeps one `webhook-id` across all retries (to use as an idempotency key), updates the timestamp on each attempt, and signs with HMAC-SHA256 over id, timestamp and body. Sources: [Stripe, Webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks), [Standard Webhooks spec](https://github.com/standard-webhooks/standard-webhooks/blob/main/spec/standard-webhooks.md).
- **Redelivery and test events.** GitHub keeps a 3-day list of recent deliveries with manual and API redelivery, plus a `ping` event. Stripe offers "Resend" in the dashboard and `stripe trigger <event>` for sandbox test events. Sources: [GitHub, Redelivering webhooks](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/testing-and-troubleshooting-webhooks/redelivering-webhooks), [GitHub, Testing webhooks](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/testing-and-troubleshooting-webhooks/testing-webhooks), [Stripe, Developers Dashboard](https://docs.stripe.com/development/dashboard).
- **Docs from source.** Keep one OpenAPI document as the single source of truth, and generate the reference, SDKs and mock servers from it so docs cannot drift from the code. Sources: [OpenAPI Initiative, Best practices](https://learn.openapis.org/best-practices.html), [Bump.sh, Code-first OpenAPI](https://bump.sh/blog/code-first-openapi/).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Partner portal: separate sandbox and live keys, a roll action with a chosen overlap (default 7 days), last-used shown for each key, and scopes (booking, availability, webhooks; clinical scopes behind a BAA or DPA). Rate limits return 429 with headers. When a partner abuses the API, we slow them further and raise an ops alert, and only a human can suspend a partner. Webhooks follow Standard Webhooks signing, with a Stripe-like 3-day backoff. An endpoint then moves to "Failing" (and is disabled after 3 days), shows a delivery log, and offers per-event and bulk "redeliver since T". Add a "Send test event" button for each event type. Generate the docs from the server's OpenAPI in CI, and never put PHI in webhook payloads (send IDs and let the partner fetch the data with a scoped key).

## 8. Accessibility baseline

- **Legal floor.** The HHS Section 504 rule requires WCAG 2.1 AA for recipients of HHS funding. In May 2026 the deadline moved to 11 May 2027 (15+ employees) and 10 May 2028 (smaller). WCAG 2.2 AA is the current W3C baseline, and building to it covers 2.1. Sources: [HHS press release, deadline extension](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-extends-mobile-and-web-accessibility-deadline.html), [Deque, Section 504 deadlines](https://www.deque.com/blog/section-504-digital-accessibility-deadlines-what-every-hhs-funded-provider-must-know/).
- **Most-missed items.** Low-contrast text appears on 83.9% of home pages (WebAIM Million 2026, up from 79.1% in 2025). The other recurring failures are missing alt text, missing form labels, empty links and buttons, and missing document language. Sources: [WebAIM Million 2026](https://webaim.org/projects/million/), [WebAIM Million 2025](https://webaim.org/projects/million/2025).
- **WCAG 2.2 additions that health apps miss.** 2.5.8 Target Size (24x24 CSS px minimum, or equivalent spacing). 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (sticky headers, bottom sheets and cookie bars must not hide focus). 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (allow paste, password managers and OTP autofill, with no cognitive puzzle). 3.3.7 Redundant Entry. 3.2.6 Consistent Help. Sources: [W3C, What's new in WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/), [Deque, WCAG 2.2](https://dequeuniversity.com/resources/wcag-2.2/).
- **Reduced motion.** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and remove motion that interaction triggers (parallax, sliding transitions). Crossfades and opacity changes are fine. This is AAA (2.3.3), but it costs little and matters for anxious or vestibular users. Sources: [W3C, Understanding 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html), [W3C technique C39](https://w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C39).
- **Arabic accessibility.** Set `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"` on the root, and mark mixed-direction runs (English names, emails, numbers) with `dir` or `<bdi>` so screen readers and bidi work. DOM and focus order must follow the RTL reading order. Allow for diacritics in line-height and in the target size of stacked controls. Screen reader support (VoiceOver, TalkBack) is also part of the crisis-feature criteria. Sources: [W3C i18n, Structural markup and RTL text](https://www.w3.org/International/questions/qa-html-dir), [W3C ALReq](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/), [Frontiers MHACSAF](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1814547/full).

**What this means for 24Therapy.** Put these in the token and component layer, not in per-screen fixes. Contrast-checked color tokens in both themes. A 44px minimum hit area on touch (above the 24px floor). A focus ring that `scroll-padding` keeps clear of sticky headers and the bottom crisis bar. OTP inputs with `autocomplete="one-time-code"` and paste allowed. A motion token that becomes 0 under reduced motion. Logical CSS properties (`margin-inline-start`) everywhere. Test every release with TalkBack in Arabic on a mid-range Android phone, since that is the main Egyptian device profile, and with VoiceOver on iOS. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA from day one so US Section 504 and future Egyptian or EU requirements need no rework.
